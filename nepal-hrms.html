Nepal HRMS

Employee self-service, attendance, leave, and payroll for Nepal.

Nepal HRMS is part of the Nepal Compliance roadmap. Its expanded HR and payroll capabilities are currently being tested in a separate app built on Frappe HR. The plan is to bring that code into Nepal Compliance after it passes beta testing.

In beta now. These capabilities are not yet included in the released Nepal Compliance app. A release date has not been announced.

Features in testing

HR workflows for employees and teams

The current beta combines an employee app with attendance, leave, and payroll tools. Access and policy behavior depend on your configuration.

Employee self-service

An installable mobile web app for check-in, leave requests, expenses, salary slips, and request status. Employees can record normal attendance or out-of-office work and review their own records.

Attendance and approvals

A live attendance board groups people by department and status. Late-entry, early-exit, and out-of-office requests give managers a way to review exceptions and approve work time.

Leave management

View balances, holidays, and leave requests. Calendar selection supports separate requests for nonconsecutive days, while draft requests are included in the available-balance calculation.

Nepal payroll

Configure salary components, Social Security Fund (SSF), provident fund, gratuity, allowances, and attendance deductions. The beta includes salary-slip explanations and scheduled payroll preparation.

Attendance reports

Compare worked and target hours, inspect daily attendance and related requests, and export spreadsheets. Nepali-calendar periods support monthly attendance reporting and payroll-period setup.

Settings and permissions

Configure grace periods, attendance exceptions, leave rules, and payroll settings. Role controls determine access to live boards, historical attendance, and other employees’ calendars.

Availability

Beta testing before inclusion in Nepal Compliance

The beta is being developed as a separate app so its HR and payroll changes can be tested before the code is brought into Nepal Compliance. This page previews that work; it does not describe capabilities already shipped in the current Nepal Compliance release.

The current beta documentation targets Frappe v15, ERPNext, Frappe HR v15, and a compatible Nepal Compliance setup. Settings and payroll formulas may change during testing. Existing HR and payroll customizations will need compatibility review before rollout.

Questions about Nepal HRMS

Is Nepal HRMS part of Nepal Compliance?

Yes. It is part of the Nepal Compliance roadmap. Development and beta testing currently happen in a separate app, with the code planned for inclusion in Nepal Compliance after testing.

Can I use these features in the released app today?

This page previews the expanded capabilities still in beta. See the current Nepal Compliance feature guides for the existing app, and check your installed version.

When will it be available?

It is coming after beta testing. No release date has been announced.

Does it replace Frappe HR?

Nepal HRMS builds on Frappe HR, adding local HR and payroll workflows and an employee-facing mobile web app.
