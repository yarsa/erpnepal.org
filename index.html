05 / Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this a separate ERP system?+

No. Nepal Compliance is an app installed on ERPNext and Frappe HR. It adds Nepal-specific functionality to those systems.

What will it cost to use?+

The software has no license fee. You arrange hosting, implementation, backups, maintenance, and any professional support separately. Their costs depend on your setup and provider.

Does it guarantee regulatory compliance?+

The app includes features intended for Nepal’s accounting and payroll requirements. Compliance also depends on your version, configuration, business practices, and current rules. Confirm your setup with a qualified local professional. This website does not claim regulatory certification.

Does it support biometric attendance devices?+

Attendance devices need a separate integration and a compatibility check for your exact model. Explore attendance device integrations before choosing equipment.

Where can I get help?+

Ask questions in GitHub Discussions or report reproducible problems in Issues. These are community channels; the project does not advertise a support response time here.
