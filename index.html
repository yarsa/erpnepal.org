A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is Nepal Compliance?+

Nepal Compliance is an open-source app that extends ERPNext and Frappe HR with Nepal-specific accounting, payroll, HR, and Bikram Sambat date workflows. It is developed in the Yarsa GitHub organization.

Is the software free?+

Yes. Nepal Compliance is available under GPL-3.0, with no software license fee. Hosting, implementation, backups, maintenance, and any professional support you choose may have separate costs.

Can I use it with an existing ERPNext installation?+

Yes. The manual installation guide covers adding the app to a working Frappe site with ERPNext and Frappe HR installed. Check the project’s current requirements and test on a backup or staging site before changing your live system.

Does it guarantee regulatory compliance?+

The app provides features designed for Nepal’s accounting and payroll workflows, including VAT reporting and CBMS integration. Compliance depends on your app version, configuration, business practices, and current requirements. Confirm your setup with a qualified local professional; this website does not claim regulatory certification.

Does it support biometric attendance devices?+

Biometric attendance compatibility is listed as planned in the project’s feature checklist. It is not presented as an available Nepal Compliance feature. Check the repository for the latest progress.

Where can I get help or contribute?+

Ask questions in GitHub Discussions, report reproducible problems in Issues, or read the contribution guide to help improve the project.
